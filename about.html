ABOUT RINGSTEAD CINEMA

Cinema in the heart of the village

Ringstead Cinema is a charity-run cinema night held in Ringstead Village Hall. We show a film on the first Friday of each month at 7.30pm.

More than a film

It's an opportunity to enjoy a good film in good company, right here in the village. Admission is £5 and includes tea or coffee. We also sell ice creams during the interval and hold a raffle each month.

Come along

Whether you've been coming for years or are joining us for the first time, you'll be very welcome.
