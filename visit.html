YOUR VISIT

A warm welcome awaits

Ringstead Cinema is a simple, friendly village cinema night at Ringstead Village Hall. Come along, settle in and enjoy a film with neighbours and friends.

When

First Friday of each month
Films start at 7.30pm.

Admission

£5 including tea or coffee.

Ice creams are available during the interval, and there is a raffle every month.

Where

Ringstead Village Hall
High Street
Ringstead
NN14 4DA

Parking

Street parking is available, with additional parking in the two lay-bys at the crossroads.
